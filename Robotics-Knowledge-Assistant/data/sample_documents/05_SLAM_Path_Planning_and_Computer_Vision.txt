5.1 Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM)
SLAM addresses the coupled chicken-and-egg problem in which a mobile robot must build a map of an unknown environment while simultaneously estimating its own trajectory within that map.
- Probabilistic Formulation: Maintain a belief over robot pose x_t and map landmarks m given control inputs u_{1:t} and sensor observations z_{1:t}.
- EKF-SLAM & FastSLAM: Early Kalman-filter and Rao-Blackwellized particle-filter approaches for landmark and 2D occupancy-grid mapping (GMapping).
- Graph-Based SLAM (SLAM Toolbox, Cartographer, ORB-SLAM3): Represents robot poses as graph nodes and odometry/scan-matching constraints as edges. Loop closure detection recognizes previously visited locations and optimizes the pose graph via nonlinear least squares (g2o, Ceres) to eliminate accumulated drift.

5.2 Robot Path Planning Algorithms
Motion planning determines collision-free trajectories from start configuration q_start to goal configuration q_goal in Configuration Space (C-Space):
- Graph Search (Dijkstra and A*): A* guarantees optimal shortest paths on discretized grids when paired with an admissible heuristic h(n) <= true_cost(n), such as Euclidean or Manhattan distance.
- Sampling-Based Planners (PRM and RRT / RRT*): Rapidly-exploring Random Trees (RRT) expand a tree by randomly sampling C-Space and steering toward valid states, making them effective in high-dimensional 6-DOF manipulator planning. RRT* rewires nearby vertices to achieve asymptotic optimality.
- Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) & Artificial Potential Fields: Reactive local planners balancing goal attraction against obstacle repulsion and motor acceleration limits.

5.3 Computer Vision and AI in Robotics
Vision pipelines give robots semantic scene understanding and 3D spatial perception:
- Classical Vision (OpenCV): Camera calibration (pinhole intrinsics K and radial/tangential distortion coefficients), AprilTag/ArUco fiducial pose estimation (SolvePnP), optical flow, and HSV/edge segmentation.
- Deep Learning Perception: Convolutional object detectors (YOLO) locate graspable targets in 2D image coordinates; RGB-D depth alignment (Intel RealSense, ZED stereo) back-projects pixel detections into 3D camera coordinates (X, Y, Z) for robotic pick-and-place.
- Reinforcement Learning and Sim-to-Real: Training locomotion or grasping policies in physics simulators (Isaac Gym, Gazebo, MuJoCo) using domain randomization before transferring policies to physical hardware.
